## Description (EN)
DraftRewind keeps every version of your thesis, homework or paper, automatically, and lets you rewind to any of them. It works on its own on your iPhone, and together with the free Windows app if you have a computer.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Every save becomes a save point with a readable title, such as "Methods: 1 paragraph added (+120 words)".
• See exactly what changed, word by word, in colour; open, share or restore any old version.
• Edit in Word or Pages; edit text and code files right in the app.
• Add photos and files from your phone: whiteboards, lab notes, scanned pages.
• Send a "what changed" PDF report to your advisor in one tap.
• Writing streak, words today and a weekly chart keep you going.
• Optional on-device summaries with Apple Intelligence: your text never leaves the phone.

YOUR CHOICE OF STORAGE
Keep a project only on this device, or connect it to iCloud Drive, Google Drive, GitHub, or several at once. Every device keeps the full history and they stay in sync: start on your phone on Monday, continue on your computer on Tuesday. If you lose a device, your project comes back with its history from the cloud. Deleted projects wait 30 days before they are gone for good.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
There is no DraftRewind server. Your files live on your device and in your own accounts. No analytics, no tracking, no account with us.

Free to use on this device. DraftRewind Pro (one-time purchase, no subscription) adds sync with your computer and backup to iCloud Drive, Google Drive and GitHub. The Windows app is free at draftrewind.com.

```
No sign-in or demo account is needed. DraftRewind keeps the version history of the user's own documents and has no server or account of ours.

TRY THE APP (no account)
On the first screen tap "Continue without an account". Then: create a project, add a file with "+", edit it, open a save point to see the coloured diff, and try Settings › Advanced › Sync engine test.

IN-APP PURCHASE (com.draftrewind.app.pro)
Cloud connections are part of the one-time purchase "DraftRewind Pro". Open Settings › DraftRewind Pro (or tap any cloud option) and buy it with the sandbox account. "Restore purchases" is at the top of Settings and on the purchase sheet.

TEST PRO WITHOUT ANY EXTRA ACCOUNT
After the purchase, connect a project to iCloud Drive. It uses the Apple ID already signed in on the review device, so no third-party account is required. Edits sync to the app's iCloud container and the project can be restored from there.

OPTIONAL THIRD-PARTY STORAGE
Google Drive (drive.file scope, only folders the app creates) and GitHub (a private repository) are optional extra storage destinations that use the user's own account. They work the same way as iCloud Drive; any Google or GitHub account can be used. Google and GitHub sign-in exist only to reach that third-party storage, so Sign in with Apple is not required (guideline 4.8 exception).

Live Activities and Apple Intelligence features are optional and degrade gracefully on devices without them.
```
